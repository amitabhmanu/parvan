# Promoting the Mahābhārata half

Four extraction agents on the MBh edges, the first work anywhere in the project able to ask
whether a claim rests on **archetypal** text or on material the BORI editors excluded.

Two promotions, two contradictions. One contradiction is a *dīnāra*-class finding; the other
is my encoding error.

---

## PROMOTE — massed elephant corps (e.113)

This was the sole constraint flooring the entire MBh heroic narrative, and it was unattested.

The discipline that mattered: elephants appear constantly and mostly **irrelevantly** — as a
city epithet (*gajasāhvaya* = Hastināpura), as ivory, as tribute, as one named sixty-year-old
tusker. None of that is the claim. What the floor needs is the *military institution*, and
**MBh.5.152.019–022** defines it outright:

> *rathasyāsan daśa gajā gajasya daśa vājinaḥ … akṣauhiṇīti paryāyair*
>
> ten elephants to a chariot, ten horses to an elephant … which by these names is called an
> *akṣauhiṇī*

Elephants as a fixed arm beside chariot, horse and foot, inside a formal army taxonomy. In use
at **MBh.6.058.031**, *anīkaṃ daśasāhasraṃ kuñjarāṇāṃ / gajānīkam* — a ten-thousand-strong
elephant division engaged as a tactical unit. In-extent archetypal counts: *caturaṅga* 19,
*gajānīka* 34, *akṣauhiṇī* 64.

## PROMOTE — the Pāṭaliputra silence (e.107), now corroborated

This edge carries the ceiling for **both** epics. Zero hits across 123,372 verses (91,573
archetypal-only) for `pāṭaliputr`, `pāṭalipur`, `kusumapur`, `puṣpapur` — twelve searches,
plain, archetypal-only and folded.

What lifts it above an ordinary argument from absence is the **positive control**. Magadha's
*older* capital is named repeatedly and unambiguously:

> **MBh.2.013.062** *tena ruddhā hi rājānaḥ sarve jitvā girivraje* — Girivraja as
> Jarāsaṃdha's seat, where he holds conquered kings captive
>
> **MBh.3.082.089** *tato rājagṛhaṃ gacchet tīrthasevī* — Rājagṛha again, in the *tīrtha*
> catalogue

**The epic's Magadhan geography is not undescribed. It is described, and stops one city
short.** Silence from a text that names the predecessor capital is far stronger than silence
from a text that names no Magadhan capital at all — and §11 scores this row **2**, a rating
that cannot see the difference.

Confidence raised 0.4 → 0.7. Two generic *rājagṛha* hits meaning "the king's house" were read
and rejected.

## CONTRADICTS — Greek astrological vocabulary (e.115), scored 4

**None of the three terms occurs anywhere in the Mahābhārata**, archetypal or apparatus, under
any spelling.

| stem | raw | what they actually are |
|---|---|---|
| `horā` | 363 | *ghorā* "terrible", *ahorātra* "day-and-night", *kaṭhorā* "harsh" |
| `\bhorā` | 3 | all elided *ahorātra* — `te 'horātravido`, `so 'horātraḥ` |
| `kendr` | 4 | sandhi artefacts: *janaka+indra*, *baka+indra*, *eka+indriya* |
| `drekkāṇ` | **0** | zero under every spelling tried |

Note the shape of this: 363 raw hits for a term that occurs zero times. A count alone would
have "confirmed" the claim handsomely. Only reading the hits shows every one is a native
Sanskrit word.

**Removed from the store**, with `ref.greek-astrology-terms` and `hor.greek-astrology`.

The fair statement about the source: §11's row names **no terms**; §3 supplies *horā*,
*kendra*, *drekkāṇa* as Greek loans into Sanskrit *jyotiṣa* generally — a claim about the
language, not about this epic. So the row cannot be checked as the document states it, and the
obvious candidates fail. That is weaker than "the document is wrong", and it is what the
evidence supports.

**Consequence:** `ws.mbh.carvaka` loosens from [150 CE, 450 CE] to **[200 BCE, 450 CE]**. The
didactic mass loses 350 years of floor, because the floor it had was not there.

## CONTRADICTS — *eḍūka* polemic (e.112). My error, not the document's.

*eḍūk* returns **zero** across MBh.12–13, the extent I gave it. Its only two occurrences in
the whole epic are **MBh.3.188**, Mārkaṇḍeya's Kali Yuga description in the Vana Parva — both
archetypal.

§11 says "on the Kali Yuga material" and names no book. I guessed the didactic mass. Retargeted
to `ws.mbh.ramopakhyana`.

**A qualification the promotion should not hide.** §3 glosses this row *"Anti-Buddhist"*. The
passage names no Buddhist:

```
MBh.3.188   bauddha 0   śramaṇa 0   nagnaka 0
            buddh   4 — every one of them buddhi, "mind"
```

What it says is *eḍūkān pūjayiṣyanti varjayiṣyanti devatāḥ* — "they will worship *eḍūkas* and
abandon the gods" — inside an undifferentiated list of degeneracy signs beside śūdras teaching
dharma and untimely rain. Reading *eḍūka* as a Buddhist reliquary mound is **an identification
the text does not make**, so the c. 250 BCE floor rests on that identification rather than on
the verse. Confidence 0.6.
