Ram.2.103.023  etac caivobhayaṃ śrutvā samyak saṃpaśya rāghava / uttiṣṭha tvaṃ mahābāho māṃ ca spṛśa tathodakam
Ram.2.103.024  athotthāya jalaṃ spṛṣṭvā bharato vākyam abravīt / śṛṇvantu me pariṣado mantriṇaḥ śreṇayas tathā
Ram.2.103.025  na yāce pitaraṃ rājyaṃ nānuśāsāmi mātaram / āryaṃ paramadharmajñam abhijānāmi rāghavam
Ram.2.103.026  yadi tv avaśyaṃ vastavyaṃ kartavyaṃ ca pitur vacaḥ / aham eva nivatsyāmi caturdaśa vane samāḥ
Ram.2.103.027  dharmātmā tasya tathyena bhrātur vākyena vismitaḥ / uvāca rāmaḥ saṃprekṣya paurajānapadaṃ janam
Ram.2.103.028  vikrītam āhitaṃ krītaṃ yat pitrā jīvatā mama / na tal lopayituṃ śakyaṃ mayā vā bharatena vā
Ram.2.103.029  upadhir na mayā kāryo vanavāse jugupsitaḥ / yuktam uktaṃ ca kaikeyyā pitrā me sukṛtaṃ kṛtam
Ram.2.103.030  jānāmi bharataṃ kṣāntaṃ gurusatkārakāriṇam / sarvam evātra kalyāṇaṃ satyasaṃdhe mahātmani
Ram.2.103.031  anena dharmaśīlena vanāt pratyāgataḥ punaḥ / bhrātrā saha bhaviṣyāmi pṛthivyāḥ patir uttamaḥ
Ram.2.103.032  vṛto rājā hi kaikeyyā mayā tad vacanaṃ kṛtam / anṛtān mocayānena pitaraṃ taṃ mahīpatim
